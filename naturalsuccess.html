01 / WHERE THINGS STAND

The front end is carrying all the weight

The $47 coaching video funnel is running between .8 and 1.3 ROAS, and scaling spend only pushes that number down further. There's currently no upsell path after the initial purchase, no order bumps, and no structured way to move buyers into higher ticket offers. That means the front end has to convert extremely efficiently just to break even, and it has almost no room to absorb rising CPMs as spend increases.

02 / WHAT I'D IMPLEMENT

Four changes, in order of speed to ship

Each one adds revenue per customer without needing a single additional visitor to the funnel.

01
VSL testing at the front end

Build out a few different VSL variations leading into the $47 offer and run them head to head. Different hooks, different proof points, different lengths. Once we know which one converts best, that becomes the control everything else gets tested against.

02
Order bumps at checkout

Add a low friction, low price add on at the point of purchase. One of the fastest ways to lift average order value without touching ad spend or conversion rate on the main offer.

03
Post purchase upsell sequence

Right after the $47 purchase, introduce one or two upsells before the customer ever leaves the checkout flow. This is where most of the missing margin is sitting right now.

04
Backend call booking into membership

Build a structured path where buyers get invited to book a call with a coach, built specifically to sell into the freshman membership. Turns a one time $47 buyer into the start of a real customer journey.

03 / WHY IT MATTERS

A higher ceiling on blended ROAS

Once order bumps, upsells, and the membership path are in place, the funnel can carry a much higher blended ROAS even if the front end conversion rate stays exactly the same. That's what makes scaling spend sustainable instead of something that quietly kills the numbers the more you push it.

04 / FIRST 30 DAYS

Suggested rollout

WEEK 1
Audit current funnel, checkout flow, and existing creative assets.
WEEK 1–2
Build and launch 3 to 4 VSL variations to find the new control.
WEEK 2
Implement order bump and first upsell in the checkout flow.
WEEK 3
Stand up the coach call booking flow and membership pitch.
WEEK 4
Review blended ROAS, tighten what's underperforming, begin scaling spend.
